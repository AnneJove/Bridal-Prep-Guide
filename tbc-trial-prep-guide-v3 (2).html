Skin Care

Glowing makeup starts with the skin underneath. Here's the short version.

Daily, non-negotiable

  • Gentle, non-stripping cleanser
  • Vitamin C serum + hyaluronic acid
  • Moisturizer to lock in hydration
  • SPF every morning, even when cloudy
  • Gentle exfoliation, 2x a week

Optional, but worth it

  • Microneedling, no later than 3–4 weeks before the wedding, done by your esthetician
Avoid within 2–3 weeks of your trial Lasers, peels, microneedling, AVOID RETINOIDS, strong acids, microblading, or anything invasive. Flaky, healing skin makes makeup look cakey.

If you are (or recently were) on oral acne medication, a hydrafacial and hydrating skincare will help balance the drying effects, so your skin is in the best shape for your trial.

Please let your esthetician know if you are on retinoids or acne medications when you book your appointment.

Hair Care

Clean, dry hair styles best. Here's what to know before you arrive.

Arriving for your appointment

  • Clean, dry hair, washed within 12 hours, no product
  • Bring any veil or hair accessories with you

Natural hair (curl types 3A–4C+)

  • We recommend a silk press or blowout before your trial and wedding
  • Want to keep your natural curls? Send us 3 photos: freshly washed, styled by you, and your inspiration photo (similar texture and length)

Please email us directly with any questions.

Clip-in extensions

  • Bring them to your trial and your wedding day
  • 100% Remy human hair, matched to your natural color
  • Brand new? Shampoo and air dry before use, once the color match is confirmed

Spray Tan / Tanning

Getting tanned for the wedding? A few things to know first.

Before your trial

  • Come to your trial already tanned, so we can match your wedding day skin
  • Keep the face tan light
  • Shower 2–3 times before your appointment
No Shade Spray Tan
Call or text Instagram
Skip the sun before your trial Sunburned skin gets dehydrated and flaky so makeup won't sit smoothly or last. Even once the redness fades, the texture often photographs rough or uneven a spray tan or self-tanner gives a controlled, camera-ready glow instead. Just don't forget your SPF along the way.
